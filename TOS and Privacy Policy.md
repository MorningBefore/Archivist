# Privacy Policy

**Bot:** Archivist
**Operator:** Aemoss
**Effective date:** November 11, 2026

Archivist is a private, personal Discord bot used only by its operator and anyone the operator explicitly authorizes. This policy explains what data the bot handles and why.

## 1. What data the bot collects
The bot only processes data from **authorized users**. Messages from anyone else are ignored and not stored.

For authorized users, the bot may process:
- **Discord identifiers:** user ID, username, and server/channel/thread IDs, used to check access and keep track of conversations.
- **Message content:** messages sent to the bot (direct messages, or messages that mention it).
- **Attachments:** files you send the bot, such as documents and images.

The bot **does not** collect passwords or payment information, and it doesn't read messages that aren't directed at it.

## 2. How the data is used
Data is used only to:
- respond to your requests;
- keep conversation history, so the bot has context across messages;
- carry out tasks you ask for (for example, creating or editing notes).

The data is **never sold**, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.

## 3. Where the data goes
- **Self-hosted storage:** conversation history, logs and files are stored on the operator's own computer.
- **AI model provider:** to generate responses, message content is sent to a third-party AI model provider (Anthropic). It is handled under that provider's own privacy policy and terms.
- **Discord:** messages pass through Discord and are subject to [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **Other services:** if you ask the bot to do something that needs another service (such as a web search or a cloud file sync), the data that task needs is sent to that service.

## 4. Data retention
- Conversation history and logs stay on the operator's computer until the operator deletes them.
- Authorized users can ask for their data to be deleted at any time (see Contact).

## 5. Security
Access to the bot is restricted to an allowlist of authorized users. The system's credentials are stored privately on the operator's computer. No system is perfectly secure, but reasonable steps are taken to protect the data.

## 6. Children
The bot is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age for Discord in your country.

## 7. Changes
This policy may be updated at any time. Changes take effect when they are published in this repository, and the effective date above will be updated.

## 8. Contact
To ask a question or request that your data be deleted: A3moss on Discord
